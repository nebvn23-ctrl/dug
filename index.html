Spring · his own rhythm

A world without instructions

In his imagined universe, he is the autistic version of a dog, experiencing everything through his own rhythms, routines, and quiet fascinations.

While other dogs chase tennis balls, DUG investigates the same patch of grass as if it holds the answer to existence.

Summer · the routine

The same little path

His favorite spot is a green hill where the sunlight warms his fluffy fur and nobody asks him to be different.

Every morning, he follows the same little path, checking that his favorite stones are exactly where he left them.

Autumn · the missing stone

The greatest adventure of a very small life

One afternoon, he finds a stone missing and sets off.

He searches beneath fences, befriends a suspicious leaf, and accidentally becomes the leader of three equally confused puppies. When they finally find the stone beside a sleeping cat, DUG realizes that even an unexpected journey can lead somewhere good.

Late autumn · the other DUGs

Not every DUG chose the quiet hill

Somewhere past the fences, other DUGs found much louder adventures.

Trenches, frontlines and burning cities. Same fur, same stare, very different routines. Our DUG watched the sky light up from his hill, then went back to checking his stones.

Winter · back on the hill

No intention of anything adventurous tomorrow

He returns to his hill with his stone, his new friends, and absolutely no intention of doing anything adventurous tomorrow.

DUG never set out to become a legend, but sometimes the world needs a little dog who reminds it that there is more than one way to belong.
